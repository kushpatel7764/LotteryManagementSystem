Lottery Management System

In short

Your lottery sales data is stored in a database file on your own computer, not on a server we run. If you turn on Google Drive backup in Settings, this app can only create, read, and delete the backup files it makes itself, inside a folder named Lottery Management Backups — it cannot see, open, or change anything else already in your Drive.

Where your data lives

1. Data stored on your computer

Lottery Management System keeps a local SQLite database on the computer it's installed on — book activations, ticket scans, daily sales reports, and your business profile. Between sessions, that database is encrypted at rest with a key that is generated on your own machine and never leaves it. We do not operate a server that this data is sent to, and we cannot see it.

Optional, off by default

2. Google Drive backup

From Settings, you can connect your own Google account so the app can back up your database file to your own Google Drive. This is opt-in: nothing is sent anywhere until you click Connect Google Drive and complete Google's sign-in screen.

Once connected, a backup runs in two situations:

  • Automatically, right after you submit a day's sales report.
  • Manually, whenever you click Back Up Now in Settings.

Each backup uploads a timestamped copy of your database directly from your computer to your Drive, into a folder the app creates called Lottery Management Backups.

Exactly what we ask Google for

3. Permissions requested

When you connect Google Drive, Google's consent screen grants us three narrow permissions:

ScopeWhat it lets us do
drive.file See, create, and delete only the specific files this app creates in your Drive — never your existing files or folders.
userinfo.email Read the email address of the connected account, so Settings can show you which account is linked.
openid Confirm your identity as part of Google's standard sign-in process.

Limits on what we can touch

4. What this app does not do

  • It cannot read, list, or modify any file in your Drive that it didn't create.
  • It does not read your email, contacts, calendar, or any other Google service.
  • It does not include analytics, ad tracking, or any third-party tracking scripts.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • Your email address is used only to display which account is connected — it is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Who's holding it, and for how long

5. Retention and your control

Backup files live in your own Google Drive, under your account's storage and your account's control — the same as any file you upload yourself. We keep no copy. You can open the Lottery Management Backups folder in Drive at any time to review, download, or delete individual backups.

Two ways to disconnect

6. Revoking access

  • In the app: open Settings → Google Drive Backup and click Disconnect.
  • In Google: visit your Google Account's third-party access page and remove Lottery Management System.

Either method immediately stops future backups. Files already uploaded to your Drive are unaffected — delete them from Drive directly if you want them gone too.

Who else is involved

7. Google's role

Connecting Google Drive means Google processes the sign-in and the file storage on its own infrastructure, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Our use of the data we receive through Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How access is protected

8. Security

Connecting your account uses Google's standard OAuth 2.0 flow — your Google password is never entered into or seen by this app. The resulting access token is stored only in a local file on your computer, alongside your database, and is never transmitted to us.

Age scope

9. Children's privacy

This software is a point-of-sale tool for lottery retailers and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.

Staying current
